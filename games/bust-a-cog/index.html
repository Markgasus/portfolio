COUNTRY CLUB FIELD GUIDE

Give the Cogs the boot.

  1. Aim and fire. Move your mouse and click the green. On a touchscreen, drag to aim and release to shoot. You can also use the arrow keys and Space.
  2. Make a match. Connect at least three balls of the same color. Anything left hanging falls away.
  3. Clear every Cog. The balls with grinning Cog faces are your targets. You do not have to clear the whole board.

Red flame balls award a bomb. Blue question balls award a wild ball. Match them like regular red or blue balls. Steel balls resist matches and bombs, but can still fall.

Challenge: clear four boards in two minutes. The board drops every ten seconds. If it reaches the tee, the run ends. Practice lets you choose a board and turn the moving board on or off.

P or Escape pauses. Leaving this tab pauses automatically. Your best completed challenge is saved in this browser.
